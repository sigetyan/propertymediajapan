Hotel photography

Photos that match the room rate

Niseko draws guests from across Asia and Australia, and many of its hotels and residences sit at the top of the market. The photos have to match the room rate.

We photograph hotels, lodges and serviced residences in Niseko and across Hokkaido: guest rooms, restaurants, lobbies, onsen and the building itself. The aim is images that show what a stay feels like, at the standard booking platforms and travel press expect.

We plan each shoot around the light and the season. In Niseko the snow, a clear sky and the hour after sunset do a lot of the work, so we book exteriors for the right day rather than the first free one.

How it works

From brief to delivery

01 Brief

Send the rooms, outlets and spaces you need, any brand guidelines and your preferred dates.

02 Plan

We plan the shoot around the light and the season, and book exteriors and drone for the right day.

03 Shoot

Rooms are styled and lit to show size, bed and view. Food, drink and restaurant spaces are shot for menus, OTAs and press.

Photos are delivered from 48 hours after the shoot, in the sizes Booking.com, Expedia and press outlets ask for.
