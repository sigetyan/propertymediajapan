Why video

Why listings need video

Photography captures a moment. Video moves through a space, shows how rooms connect, and gives buyers a real sense of scale and flow. For a buyer in Singapore or Sydney comparing Niseko properties they cannot visit in person, a two-minute walkthrough answers many of the questions a viewing would.

Video also travels further. A short clip gets shared on social media and messaging apps in a way a photo gallery rarely does.

Before the shoot we go through the property with the agent and agree what the video needs to show. The edit is built around those points.

What we produce

Three types of property video

Property walkthrough

A smooth, cinematic move through the property from entrance to living areas, bedrooms, bathrooms and outdoor spaces. The standard format for residential listings. Edited to one to two minutes with music and colour grading.

Delivery: 48 hours  |  Format: MP4, 1080p or 4K

Aerial and ground combo

Ground footage inside and out, combined with drone shots flown under JCAB rules. It shows the property in its Niseko setting: ski access, mountain views, land size and distance to the village. The most complete format for premium listings.

Delivery: 48 hours  |  Requires: JCAB clearance if applicable

Short-form social cut

A 30 to 60 second vertical cut optimised for Instagram Reels, TikTok and LINE. Produced from the same footage as the main walkthrough at no additional shoot cost. Formatted for viewing on a phone.

Delivery: 48 hours  |  Format: Vertical 9:16, MP4
How it works

How a video shoot works

We keep the production process straightforward. No lengthy pre-production meetings or complicated approval chains. You brief us, we shoot, we edit, you receive the finished file.

Brief and booking

Fill in the enquiry form with the property address, your preferred shoot date and any specific shots you want included. We usually confirm availability and pricing the same business day.

Pre-shoot prep

We walk through the property brief with you before the shoot day. If you have shots in mind, tell us and we will plan them in. We plan the shot sequence to make the most of available time.

Shoot day

A standard residential video shoot takes two to three hours. Aerial add-ons are scheduled to coincide with suitable weather and light conditions, in line with JCAB requirements.

Edit and delivery

The finished video is usually delivered via download link within 48 hours. Files are formatted for portal upload, social media and direct sharing. One round of minor revisions is included.

What you receive

What you receive

Every video delivery includes everything you need to publish immediately across portals, social media and your agency website.

  • Main listing video in MP4 format, colour graded and music licensed for commercial use
  • Social media cut at 9:16 vertical ratio for Reels and Stories, if requested
  • 1080p and 4K versions where applicable, for portal upload and screen display
  • Download link with a 30-day access window and direct sharing capability
  • One revision round included for minor timing or music adjustments
