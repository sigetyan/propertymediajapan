Makkari House is a multi level holiday rental on a large lot below Mount Yotei. It has four bedrooms, two bathrooms, two living areas including a tatami room, an open kitchen and dining area, balconies, a garden and a single car garage.

The listing was aimed at guests, so we focused on how the house lives: the mountain behind the exterior, natural light through the big windows, and the traditional rooms that set it apart from other rentals. A walkthrough video sits alongside the photos.
