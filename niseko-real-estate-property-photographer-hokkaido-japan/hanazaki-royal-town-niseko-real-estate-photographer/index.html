Hanazaki Royal Town is a new residential development in the Hanazaki area of Niseko. We photographed one of its finished homes in summer 2025, with the forest around it in full leaf.

The brief was a full listing set: the front elevation among the trees, the living room and its forest view, the kitchen and dining area, three bedrooms and the stone tiled bathrooms. The big windows were the selling point, so most interiors are composed to keep the trees in frame.
