Hikari House is a two unit townhouse in Hirafu village, completed in 2021. Each unit has two bedrooms, two bathrooms, an open plan living area with high ceilings and a kitchen with a central island, with off street parking outside.

We shot the exterior at dusk so the timber and metal cladding and the lit windows would stand out, then covered both units inside. We also produced 3D floorplans of the units and the site, so buyers can see how the two homes fit together on the lot.
