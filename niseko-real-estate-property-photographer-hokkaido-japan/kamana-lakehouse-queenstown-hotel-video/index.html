Kāmana Lakehouse is a boutique hotel on the hill above Lake Wakatipu in Queenstown, New Zealand, with lake view rooms, outdoor hot tubs and its own restaurant and bar.

Our founder filmed a 50 second brand film and eight short vertical videos for social media over one day, with models and hotel staff organised by the hotel. Each vertical clip covers one room type or one part of the hotel, from the hot tubs to brunch and evening cocktails in the restaurant.

This project was shot in New Zealand before we moved to Niseko. Hotels in Niseko and across Hokkaido can book the same kind of brand film and vertical clips. See hotel photography and video.
