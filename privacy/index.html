Privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Nomado Godo Kaisha (NOMADO合同会社, "we") runs Property Media Japan, a real estate and hotel photography service. We handle personal information in line with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) and related laws and guidelines, as set out below.

2. What we collect

We collect the following personal information by fair means.

  • When you enquire or ask for a quote: your name, company or office, email address, phone number, property address, preferred shoot date, the services you need and your message.
  • When you book: the above, plus contact details for the person on site, billing details, information we need to access the property (such as key handover) and payment details.
  • During a shoot: for real estate work we photograph the property and do not normally photograph people. If people appear in a hotel film or similar work, we agree this with the client beforehand.
  • When you visit this site: access information such as IP address, browser type and the date and time of your visit (server records).

3. How we use it

We use personal information only for these purposes.

  1. Answering enquiries and preparing and sending quotes.
  2. Providing our services: scheduling, shooting and delivering files.
  3. Invoicing, confirming payments and other transaction administration.
  4. Telling you about our services and about changes to them.
  5. Improving our services and planning new ones, using statistical information.
  6. Meeting legal obligations and resolving disputes.
  7. Running this site securely and preventing unauthorised access.
  8. With the client's agreement, showing photos and videos we have shot as examples of our work.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not give personal data to third parties without your prior consent, except where the APPI or other laws allow it. Sharing data with the service providers described in section 5, or as part of a merger or other business succession, is not treated as provision to a third party.

5. Service providers

We use outside providers for customer management, email and website delivery, and they handle personal data on our behalf only as far as needed for those purposes. We choose providers that handle personal data properly and supervise them as required.

7. How we protect personal data

We take these measures to prevent personal data being leaked, lost or damaged.

  • Policy: this privacy policy sets out how we handle personal data.
  • Handling rules: we set how personal data is collected, used, stored, shared and deleted, and who is responsible at each stage.
  • Organisation: we appoint a person responsible for personal data and keep a reporting process for any incident.
  • People: everyone who handles personal data is told how to handle it.
  • Physical security: we protect devices and papers holding personal data against theft and loss, and encrypt data that leaves the office.
  • Technical security: we limit who can access personal data and protect accounts with password management and two-step verification.
  • Understanding the environment abroad: personal data is handled in the services of the overseas companies listed in section 6, and we take measures with each country's laws in mind. With some cloud services, the country where the server storing the data sits cannot be identified. Our directors and staff may also handle personal data while working in New Zealand, where the Privacy Act 2020 applies.

8. Your requests

You or your representative can ask us to tell you the purpose of use of personal data we hold about you, to disclose it (including records of any provision to third parties), to correct, add to or delete it, to stop using or erase it, or to stop providing it to third parties. We will confirm your identity and respond without delay as the law requires. Send requests by email to the contact in section 12. We may ask for official identification to confirm your identity, and an agent should also provide a document showing their authority, such as a letter of authorisation. There is no fee. We will disclose information in the way you choose, including electronically by email; if that way would be very costly or otherwise difficult, we will provide it in writing.

9. Cookies and outside services

  • Fonts (Google Fonts)
  • YouTube videos (loaded only when you press play)
  • Some videos served from our related site, propertymedia.nz (loaded only when you press play)
  • Site delivery and security (Cloudflare)

10. How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires, and then delete or destroy it promptly.
