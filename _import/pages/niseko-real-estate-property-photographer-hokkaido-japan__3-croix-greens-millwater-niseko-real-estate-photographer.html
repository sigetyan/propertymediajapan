3 Croix Greens Millwater - Niseko Real Estate Photographer

About us:

Property Media Japan specializes in Real Estate Photography, Videography, Aerial Photography, Floor-plans and Virtual Staging. Previously based in Queenstown New Zealand, we are now operating in Niseko, Hokkaido Japan.

Key Features:

  • Bedrooms: 3 spacious bedrooms designed to maximize comfort and style, complete with ample natural lighting and tranquil views, ensuring a peaceful retreat from the busy world outside.

  • Bathrooms: 2 beautifully designed bathrooms featuring contemporary fixtures and finishes, including a master bath with sophisticated accents and a deep soaking tub for ultimate relaxation.

  • Living Room: A generously sized living room with elegant detailing, plush carpeting, and large windows that create a bright and inviting atmosphere. This space is ideal for both relaxing family nights and lively gatherings.

  • Kitchen: A modern kitchen equipped with high-end appliances, sleek cabinetry, and a functional layout that includes a spacious dining area. The kitchen's open-plan design facilitates a smooth flow into the living areas, perfect for entertaining.

  • Garage: The property includes a convenient single-car garage, providing secure parking and additional storage solutions.

  • Outdoor Living: Landscaped grounds that blend seamlessly with the natural surroundings, including a private deck perfect for enjoying the outdoors and taking in the panoramic views of Milwater Estate.

Constructed with attention to detail and high-quality materials, 3 Croix Greens Millwater offers an exceptional living experience. Whether you're seeking a family home or a luxurious retreat, this property provides the perfect combination of comfort, style, and functionality in Millwater Estate, Auckland.
